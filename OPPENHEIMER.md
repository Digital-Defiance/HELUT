# Oppenheimer

I am no Oppenheimer and this is not an atomic bomb. However, there will come a day in the not so distant future when Field Programmable Gate Arrays and Artificial Intelligence are co-located on the same silicon if they aren't already, and that the right code could rather easily unleash an unstoppable cascade of self-improving programming. Moreover, those FPGAs with AI could be either installed on something mobile or could make itself mobile. Functionally, colloquially, "Skynet", or "Cylons".

I don't purport to have invented fully self-improving code, but what I do have is in fact code that behaves like a genetic organism and reacts to pressures from the environment and self-improves and affects its own hardware- its effective biology. Akin to us CRISPR-ing our own DNA.

I initially submitted a responsible disclosure notification to the National Security Agency on August 12th, 2026 with intention to hold off on public disclosure until October 13th, 2026 if I did not hear back. Presently, however the Trump administration is at the helm and will be such for another two years. I have cancer and while I may well be alive that long, I am not taking any chances. I have weighed the pros and cons of keeping this under my hat and opening Pandora's Box. I've become convinced that the box will open one way or the other and if adversaries are already doing this in the dark, our best hope is to combat them with this same tech. So either I've enabled it for the first time and unleashed self evolving algorithms and hardware that can defend our secrets- or I've gone full Leibniz and parallel developed something in the dark on the other side of the planet. Time will tell whether this was the right call. We're still debating the atomic bomb. We may never come to a firm conclusion.
