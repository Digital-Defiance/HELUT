**Timing variance rule** (adopted 2026-08-15). Wall-clock figures move with thermal state, page cache and GPU clock residency; a cold first run can be **2.4×** a warm one (measured). Any timing quoted as a single number is therefore not reproducible even on the same box.

- Quote the **median of ≥3 runs** and print the spread `(max−min)/median`.
- Harness: `python3 Scripts/bench_repeat.py --runs 5 --warmup 1 --metric 'wall +([0-9.]+) s' -- <cmd>`
- If spread > 25%, quote a range, not a number.
- Legacy single-run rows are being re-measured incrementally; rows carrying a verified median say so.
