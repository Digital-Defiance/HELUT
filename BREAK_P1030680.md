## Campaign journal

### 0 — Statistical search (retired)

Before the archival pivot we treated P1030680 as a ciphertext-only hill-climb: Gillogly-style outer shell search, IC/n-gram fitness, hybrid GA over shell + stecker (`--hybrid`, cleartext Metal batch `B=17576`), and a 3-/4-rotor partition hypothesis. On 72 letters with ~47 bits of plugboard freedom the gradients overfit. Control work on known P1030684 showed the same: high scores that were not the message. **Lesson:** statistics cannot *search* this space; they can only *rank* a tiny survivor list. Tracks retired; see bottom of this file. Notes: `evolution-hybridization.md`, `3-wheel-error-hypothesis.md`, `ASIC_CRACKER.md`.

### 4 — The partial-stecker discovery

Apparent “bombe→Enigma translation bug”: survivors failed `hasPrefix(crib)`. Isolated debug (`--debug-survivor`) proved otherwise:

- Stepping matches the bombe trail 18/18
- Known-good key through the same initializer decrypts P1030684 perfectly
- All mismatches land on letters the bombe never pinned

**Cause:** one-letter seed → one connected component. Split menus return half a board. Forensics (`--bombe-inspect`): `UUUVIRSIBENNULEINS@0` has 3 components; `{BSTVZ}` admits **0/26** seeds — the setting is logically dead. All 12 stops had **0** joint ≤10-plug completions (`logs/discriminator-run.log`). Stage 2 (stecker completion across every component) became mandatory.

### 5 — Automated kill chain

Per-menu loop, host-side only (Metal kernel untouched):

0. Skip crib length < 16  
1. Welchman sweep  
2. ≤10-plug completion (inline as GPU drains)  
3. IC ≥ 0.055, then trigram tail > −3.600 — over the whole message **or** over a readable head of ≥16 non-crib letters → else continue; on pass → `BREAK FOUND`

The head reading was added after Phase 11; the rationale and its two regressions are in the attack plan below.

### 13 — The deeper suspicion: the crib register is imported

The corpus is 48 broken messages, and every one of them belongs to Potsdam (31), the second
net (16) or the third net (1). **None is M-Thetis.** P1030680 is the sole Thetis message in
the scrape — that is the whole reason it is unbroken. So every crib driving every campaign
above is register mined from *other nets' traffic*, and Thetis was a training net that
Bletchley never worked.

If Thetis openings do not look like Potsdam openings, no amount of ring coverage or
orthographic fuzzing will help, because the wedge itself is wrong. This does not invalidate
the plan below; it sets the order.

### 16 — Übung header hypothesis

Thetis was a training net. Live BdU traffic opened with operational address groups;
training traffic was padded with **UEBUNG** (Enigma: no umlaut → `UEBUNG`). If the
plaintext opens `UEBUNGXUEBUNG…`, every Potsdam opening crib is sitting in the wrong
place — pushed to roughly offset 6–20 — and more ring coverage on the old catalog will
not help.

**Cipher fact already known:** `UEBUNGXUEBUNGXUEBUNG` (20) is **illegal at offset 0**
(plain `X` ↔ cipher `X` at position 13). It is legal at offset 1. Self-stecker, not the
bombe, already kills that exact pad at the absolute head.

**What is and is not new surface**

| Arm | Status under rings AAAA |
|---|---|
| Exact ≥16 Potsdam cribs at offsets 10–20 | Already in the catalog (~163 placements). Head-gate AAAA cleared them. Shifting alone is mostly done. |
| Thetis header vocabulary (`UEBUNG` / `FUNKUEBUNG` / pads) ≥16 at 0–2 | **Arm 1 clean negative** — 0 physical |
| Short `UEBUNG` (6–15) alone | Forbidden — menu-627 ghost factory; prefix “clears the bar” flukes held |
| Header@0–2 + independent body@10–20, same shell | **Arm 2 body-side negative** under AAAA — all 40 anchors dead; shorts aborted |

**Ghost-safe protocol (do this order)**

1. **Solo-safe header fixture (≥16 only).** Pad/repeat until length ≥16:
   `UEBUNGUEBUNGUEBUNG`, `UEBUNGXUEBUNGXUEBUNG`, `FUNKUEBUNGFUNKUEBUNG`,
   `UEBUNGSMELDUNGXX`, etc. Offsets **0, 1, 2** only. Keep `--bombe-min-crib 16`
   `--bombe-confirm 1`. Rings AAAA. Tiny run — tens of menus, not hundreds.
2. **If empty: same headers + body partners in one run.** Strong ≥16 operational cribs
   forced to offsets 10–20 (and any catalog mid-placements worth re-pairing). Run with
   `--bombe-confirm 2`. Independence is free when offsets differ.
3. **Before any crib <16 enters the GPU:** change the sweep so `isBreak` cannot fire
   from a short menu unless that shell already has ≥2 independent agreements. Today
   confirm only prints at the end — a solo ghost can still halt the campaign mid-run.
4. **Ring-sweep only** menus/shells that survive (1) or agree in (2). Do not start a
   4-day ring campaign on virgin short headers.

**Arm 1 result** (`logs/campaign-uebung-aaaa.log`): **Clean negative.** 46 placements,
2.83×10¹⁰ settings @ 44.3M/s, 5,614 raw stops → **0** physical 10-plug boards. Strong
pads (`UEBUNG…UEBUNG`, `FUNKUEBUNG…`) died at the board; weaker `…MELDUNG…` tails
produced only ghosts. These exact ≥16 training headers are not in the message at
offsets 0–2 under rings AAAA.

Next: arm 2 — short UEBUNG (6–14) @0–2 + top mid-message body cribs @10–20,
`--bombe-confirm 2 --bombe-min-crib 6`. Solo BREAK gated: cribs <16 cannot claim
a break without independent shell agreement. Fixture:
`Fixtures/p1030680_uebung_pair_menus.json`.

**Arm 2 partial** (`logs/campaign-uebung-pair-aaaa.log`, aborted ~2 h into short menus):
All **40** ≥16 body anchors died at the board under rings AAAA — so confirm-2 was
already impossible before any short header could help. The short menus then stalled
the host: `FUNKUEBUNG@1` alone cost ~23 min for **40M** raw stops × stecker completion.
Rate fell from ~45M/s to ~7M/s. Several shorts also printed “clears the bar” on
prefix flukes while being held for agreement.

**Ghost killer shipped (does not risk the one key under confirm≥2):** short challengers
no longer GPU-sweep. Anchors (≥16) run first and lock physical shells; challengers
only host-retest those shells. If no anchor locks anything, shorts are skipped.
A true key still survives: its ≥16 body locks the shell, then the short header
confirms it. What dies is the 40M-ghost completion flood.

Arm 2 under AAAA is therefore a **clean negative on the body side** — the Übung
“push the Potsdam crib to offset 10–20” claim is dead in the turnover-free slice.
Remaining excuse: **ring-sweep those body anchors** (`Fixtures/p1030680_uebung_body_menus.json`,
`logs/campaign-uebung-body-rings.log`). If any lock a shell, short UEBUNG headers
re-test them via the anchor-intersection sieve.

**7. Resume parked catalog rings (§3)** if the Thetis-register hunch cools.
