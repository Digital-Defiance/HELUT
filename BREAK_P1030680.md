## Campaign journal

### 0 — Statistical search (retired)

Before the archival pivot we treated P1030680 as a ciphertext-only hill-climb: Gillogly-style outer shell search, IC/n-gram fitness, hybrid GA over shell + stecker (`--hybrid`, cleartext Metal batch `B=17576`), and a 3-/4-rotor partition hypothesis. On 72 letters with ~47 bits of plugboard freedom the gradients overfit. Control work on known P1030684 showed the same: high scores that were not the message. **Lesson:** statistics cannot *search* this space; they can only *rank* a tiny survivor list. Tracks retired; see bottom of this file. Notes: `evolution-hybridization.md`, `3-wheel-error-hypothesis.md`, `ASIC_CRACKER.md`.

### 1 — Archival first (history cheaper than search)

Scraped the 1 May 1945 U534 corpus (`Scripts/scrape_u534.py` → `Fixtures/u534_corpus.json`): 48 broken M4 messages, one hand-cipher, **one** unbroken Enigma — this message.

| Finding | Result |
|---|---|
| Depth partner | None — sole unbroken Enigma in the scrape |
| Kiss / re-sent plaintext | 72 overlays → 8 survivors ≈ 5.8 expected by chance — no kiss |
| Three recovered daily keys vs P1030680 | All 26⁴ message keys exhausted; noise floor (bigram ≈ −212) |
| Naval indicators as key constraint | Grund table lost → **1.92×** (~0.94 bit); net ID only |
| Thetis key sheet | Training net; BP never worked it; water-soluble sheets — nothing to find |

**What the archives did buy:** register language. `Scripts/crib_mine.py` mined formulaic cribs from the 48 decrypts and every self-encipherment-legal placement in the target → `Fixtures/p1030680_menus.json` (**100 cribs / 2,513 placements**).

### 2 — Welchman diagonal board (Boolean engine)

Built a crib-driven bombe: host `WelchmanDiagonalBoard.swift`, Metal `BombeMetal.swift` (~40M settings/s, GPU↔host **0 lane mismatches**), driver `BombeSweep.swift`.

**Search reductions (Turing-shaped, essential):** Greek never steps → pin Greek ring A (26×); left notch drives nothing → pin left ring A (26×); **676×** shell collapse, verified on `EnigmaM4Machine.step`, and since measured against a non-AAAA true key (Phase 11). Middle and right rings are absorbed into their window positions only while the span is turnover-free. `--bombe-ring-sweep` buys back the **right** ring at 26× — it does **not** cover the middle ring, which stays pinned at A in every run to date. See the coverage ledger, Phase 12.

**Rehearsal (P1030684 known key), board only:** this grades the diagonal board, not the campaign — it is *handed* the true shell and searches 26⁴ positions. The true setting is kept at every crib length; at **16 letters** the only survivor in 26⁴ is the true key; full historical stecker recovered. Crib length is everything. For the campaign path graded blind, see Phase 11.

### 4 — The partial-stecker discovery

Apparent “bombe→Enigma translation bug”: survivors failed `hasPrefix(crib)`. Isolated debug (`--debug-survivor`) proved otherwise:

- Stepping matches the bombe trail 18/18
- Known-good key through the same initializer decrypts P1030684 perfectly
- All mismatches land on letters the bombe never pinned

**Cause:** one-letter seed → one connected component. Split menus return half a board. Forensics (`--bombe-inspect`): `UUUVIRSIBENNULEINS@0` has 3 components; `{BSTVZ}` admits **0/26** seeds — the setting is logically dead. All 12 stops had **0** joint ≤10-plug completions (`logs/discriminator-run.log`). Stage 2 (stecker completion across every component) became mandatory.

### 5 — Automated kill chain

Per-menu loop, host-side only (Metal kernel untouched):

0. Skip crib length < 16  
1. Welchman sweep  
2. ≤10-plug completion (inline as GPU drains)  
3. IC ≥ 0.055, then trigram tail > −3.600 — over the whole message **or** over a readable head of ≥16 non-crib letters → else continue; on pass → `BREAK FOUND`

The head reading was added after Phase 11; the rationale and its two regressions are in the attack plan below.

### 12 — Coverage ledger (what is actually eliminated)

Two overclaims, both now fixed in the code that prints them:

1. `right ring swept: turnover phase fully covered, elimination is complete` — false. The
   sweep covers every phase of the *right*-wheel turnover, so middle-wheel stepping is
   right for all 26. The **middle ring stays pinned at A**, which is only exact while the
   middle wheel does not itself turn over in span. A key that steps the left wheel mid-crib
   is out of reach of every run to date — at the 27–40 letter spans in play that is roughly
   **8%** of keys with a one-notch middle wheel and **15%** with a two-notch one (VI–VIII).
2. "all 2,513 placements" — the catalog runs tested the **886** placements of length ≥16.
   The other 1,627 were dropped by the min-crib filter, by design, and never tested.

| Arm | Placements | Ring coverage | Status |
|---|---|---|---|

| Exact, right ring swept | **54** (union of openings-rings + top-30, 6 overlap) | all 26 right-ring phases | complete |
| Fuzzed, rings AAAA | 400 | turnover-free spans only | complete |
| Fuzzed, right ring swept | 19 of 40 so far | all 26 | menus 19–40 in flight (menu 19 dead at the board) |
| Placements < 16 letters | 1,627 | none | never tested |
| Middle ring ≠ A | — | — | never tested, any arm |

So: **54 of 886 exact placements (6%) have had real ring coverage.** The headline "clean
negative on the catalog" is a negative over ≤19% of the ring space. That is the honest
state, and it is the reason the next campaign is worth its four days.

### 13 — The deeper suspicion: the crib register is imported

The corpus is 48 broken messages, and every one of them belongs to Potsdam (31), the second
net (16) or the third net (1). **None is M-Thetis.** P1030680 is the sole Thetis message in
the scrape — that is the whole reason it is unbroken. So every crib driving every campaign
above is register mined from *other nets' traffic*, and Thetis was a training net that
Bletchley never worked.

If Thetis openings do not look like Potsdam openings, no amount of ring coverage or
orthographic fuzzing will help, because the wedge itself is wrong. This does not invalidate
the plan below; it sets the order.

## Attack plan

Measured cost basis: one placement with a full right-ring sweep = 34,944 shells ×
456,976 lanes = 1.6×10¹⁰ settings ≈ **6.5 min** at 41M/s. About 190 placements/day.

**1. Windowed discriminator score (code shipped), then re-read the survivors we already have.**
*~7 h GPU remaining. Cheapest shot at "the answer is already in the logs."*
The tail score is computed over the whole 72-letter decrypt. If the true key's middle wheel
turns over *after* the crib but inside the message, the decrypt is correct across the crib
span and garbage after it — the whole-message tail lands near the German/noise midpoint
(≈ −4.1), under the −3.600 bar, and the true key is thrown away. The control did not trip
this (its middle ring absorbed cleanly over all 119 letters), which is precisely why the
gap survived unnoticed. The AAAA catalog runs already produced **2,219 physically valid
≤10-plug boards** (2,118 + 101) rejected on tail alone. Score the crib span and the best
contiguous window alongside the full message, then re-run the AAAA catalog to re-examine
them.

*Shipped in `PostBombeDiscriminator`:* `PrefixEvidence` scans heads `plain[0..<end]` with the
crib excised, requiring `minPrefixTail = 16` non-crib letters, and the break gate now passes
on the whole message **or** a qualifying head at the same −3.600 / 0.055 bars. The IC
pre-filter had the same flaw — it dropped diluted decrypts before trigrams ever ran — and now
admits a completion whose head clears the floor. `--bombe-inspect` also scores its
completions, so forensics reports what a live board actually reads as.

Two regressions, both green:

| Regression | Expectation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Blind control (Phase 11) re-run | true key still breaks | identical break, same shell, same ten plugs, IC 0.064 / tail −2.848 |
| Menu-627 ghost `KOMXADMXUUUBOO@29` | must **not** be resurrected | dies on both readings — whole IC 0.043 / tail −4.070, best head IC 0.042 / tail −4.116 |

The ghost's head scores *worse* than its whole message, which is the point: maximising over
nested heads cannot manufacture a correct prefix where none exists.

**2. Finish the fuzzed top-40.** *~2.5 h, running now.* 22 placements, command in Phase 10.
Close the arm before opening another.

**3. Ring-sweep the exact ≥16 catalog.** *886 × 6.5 min ≈ 96 h ≈ 4 days.*
The run that turns "AAAA clean negative at ≤19% ring coverage" into an actual elimination of
the exact catalog. Everything else about exact cribs is a guess until this finishes.

**4. Unlock the 1,627 discarded short placements with multi-menu agreement.** *~7 h triage.*
A 12–15 letter crib is below unicity **alone** — that is what produced the −4.070 false
alarm. Two *independent* menus agreeing on the same shell is a different claim entirely, and
`--bombe-confirm 2` / `LockedShellAgreements` already implements it. Triage at rings AAAA,
then ring-sweep only shells with agreement. The only arm that adds new crib surface instead
of more of the same.

**5. Middle-ring sweep on the strongest placements.** *20 × 26 × 6.5 min ≈ 56 h.*
Closes the 8–15% identified in Phase 12. Worth it only after 3.

**6. Re-mine cribs for Thetis specifically, per Phase 13.**
Not more orthographic noise on Potsdam register — different register. A training net's
72-letter message plausibly carries a callsign or Kenngruppe group before the body, which
would put a *short* crib at offset 5–15 and make step 4, not step 3, the one that lands.
